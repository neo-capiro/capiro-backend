# Standard meeting preparation latency change

[Source PR #2178](https://github.com/neo-capiro/capirov2/pull/2178) increases extraction concurrency for meeting prep and changes reasoning effort only for standard-depth repairs. The October 6 RT | Capiro Onboarding recovery extracted 829 facts from 20 batches; after eight cached batches, the remaining extraction took about seven minutes. Initial synthesis took about three minutes, and coverage review plus two repairs took about nine minutes. The live reviewer reported 138 important omissions after synthesis and 82 after the first repair. For that reason, the release retains both repair passes and the full-ledger review.

Extraction concurrency rises from three to four requests per job. The first draft keeps high reasoning, while standard-depth repair requests use medium reasoning. Deep/max requests keep high reasoning throughout. Source collection, evidence budgets, provider retry bounds, absolute deadlines, saved draft/extraction checkpoints, actor/client authorization, and artifact persistence are unchanged. The worker records only job ID, stage, elapsed milliseconds, token counts, truncated status, reasoning level, and aggregate counts; it does not log prompts, source text, provider responses, credentials, or meeting content. Context preparation and saved-artifact elapsed times are also recorded.

JEV's passage-relevance task is provisionally calibrated for a different selection path. Invoking it here would add calls and could exclude important prep sources before extraction. This release does not invoke JEV. C2 Strategies' direct Krisp notes/debrief path and all other tenant notetaker flows are untouched.
