### Office-reference lifecycle and concurrency decision

Existing target mutations may rescope a target to another client, rekey a manual target to Directory, archive it, or merge and delete the manual target. Initial plan validation alone is insufficient. [Current target owner](https://github.com/neo-capiro/capiro-backend/blob/cd3c51e940f14e1b2a1040b0ffa445e85d68b332/apps/api/src/engagement/report-targets.ts#L218).

Conservative candidate: bind existing targetId plus officeKey-at-binding and clientId, revalidate on every read, mutation and recommendation, and mark a missing/archived/rescoped/rekeyed/merged reference unavailable without revealing its new client or metadata. Do not silently follow names, attach a different target, cascade a plan deletion or rewrite its client. Permit explicit versioned removal/rebinding; block new bindings or operations that rely on an unavailable reference. Whether to follow an owner-issued merge/rekey receipt later is a separate reviewed extension.

Before persistence can bind these references, coordinate one existing target-owner fence: expose/reuse the current report-target tenant transaction lock through its owner, rather than copying the SQL or importing account ownership. Every plan operation that validates or changes target references takes that fence first, then its client/actor/grant and aggregate locks. Existing target writers already take the fence; the future integration must prove lock order and revocation/rescope/rekey/merge races with them. No frozen target route is changed now, and no FK/cascade/migration decision is allocated by this proposal. Office association persistence remains gated on that owner coordination.

## 6. Stale-plan meaning and DC-window inputs

**Recommended stale meaning for review:** a review cue on an existing authorized plan whose explicitly recorded review basis is no longer valid. It is not an archive, permission change, lifecycle transition or instruction to schedule/send anything. Keep lifecycle and freshness separate.

Candidate review protocol for explicit owner acceptance: an authorized plan editor supplies a future reviewDueAt (no default TTL); a review command requires expectedPlanVersion, increments to version V+1, and records reviewedPlanVersion=V+1 and the explicit deadline in that same receipt. Prior review never suppresses expiration indefinitely: expiry still applies. A subsequent aggregate edit invalidates the exact reviewed-version basis. Under this conservative candidate, freshness is current only while the accepted review matches the current aggregate version and the deadline is still in the future; a valid prior review with version drift or an expired deadline is stale; no accepted review/basis is unknown. The owner must expressly approve who may review, whether every edit invalidates review, how expiry/reset work, and the review/version transition before any schema freeze. Until then no stale computation is exposed. No default “N days old” rule, no inference from reportYear, latest touch, unrelated client updatedAt, campaign hashes or a sample label. Basis-change criteria (Objective/profile/target change) need a separately approved list before they can invalidate review. Without an approved criterion/basis, return freshness unknown; failed/denied/partial reads never become Stale or No plan.

**Candidate recommendation inputs:**

- Exact authorized plan/wave IDs and aggregate versions; DC-eligible format and explicit allowable/request date intervals with timezone and date/instant precision.
- A source-qualified House/Senate sitting/recess calendar snapshot: source identity/URL, relevant chamber, source revision, published/checked times, covered interval, completeness and known recess intervals. Existing hearing/calendar events and the two-year Congress-number helper are not a complete sitting/recess calendar. [event-calendar shape](https://github.com/neo-capiro/capiro-backend/blob/cd3c51e940f14e1b2a1040b0ffa445e85d68b332/apps/api/src/hearing-discovery/legislative-calendar.ts#L57), [Congress-number helper](https://github.com/neo-capiro/capiro-backend/blob/cd3c51e940f14e1b2a1040b0ffa445e85d68b332/apps/api/src/common/congress.util.ts#L1).
- Explicit constraints only: requested duration/date horizon, required office/chamber kinds, already-recorded commitments and capacity/travel constraints if an admitted owner supplies them. Missing constraints are disclosed; do not invent availability, travel time or staff capacity.
- Proposed intervals retain one declared IANA timezone; exact wire end-bound semantics (recommended half-open) must be documented before implementation.
- Eligible shared trip requires overlap of each admitted wave's allowed interval and complete calendar coverage. Conservatively exclude recess and unknown/conflicting calendar days; never infer sitting from a hearing or infer office availability from sitting.
- If official calendar coverage/freshness is absent, return unavailable with reason, not an empty success or synthetic recommendation. With valid complete inputs and no eligible intersection, return a distinct complete empty result.
- Until ranking policy is reviewed, list eligible candidates chronologically as a presentation order; no Best label or universal five-week lead-time score. Explain only actual constraints/provenance. A recommendation never creates a trip, email, meeting, bot, paid job or approval.
