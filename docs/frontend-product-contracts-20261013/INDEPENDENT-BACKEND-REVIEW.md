**Decision:** Accept the bounded source, mocked-test, typecheck, and build qualification of the cached comment-deadline range endpoint. Native database, deployed authorization, publisher freshness, frontend behavior, and whole-row CORE-109/MCAL-010/PROS-024 acceptance remain open.

[Source composition](evidence/source-composition-audit.json) records 26,401 unchanged base entries, 33 exact qualified authority inputs, unchanged policy/artifact/package pins, no schema or migration change, and no consumption of queued bill donor PR2469. The [existing-body comparison](evidence/federal-register-existing-bodies.json) finds all six prior service member bodies byte-identical, including effective calendar, upcoming deadlines, and document detail.

`GET /federal-register/comment-deadline-calendar` inherits the controller's `RolesGuard` and `standard_user` requirement. It validates real `YYYY-MM-DD` dates, ordered range of at most 366 days, and bounded integer page/limit before querying. The inclusive endpoint days use a UTC next-day-exclusive filter on `commentEndDate` alone. Rows and count use the same predicate, stable `(commentEndDate, documentNumber)` order, and a `RepeatableRead` transaction with latest ingestion metadata. Missing, out-of-range, and non-midnight cached candidates are excluded without borrowing publication or effective dates; candidate counts and `hasMoreCandidates` still advance through excluded rows. Read failures propagate. Coverage remains `complete:false` and `allFederalDocuments:false` even after a successful ingestion run. The source ingester maps publisher `comments_close_on` to this cached field but does not persist the raw publisher string, so each returned row states `rawPublisherValueVerified:false`. Canonical midnight projection is not raw-format attestation.
