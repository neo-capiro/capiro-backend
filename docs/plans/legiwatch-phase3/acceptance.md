## Public-source qualification checkpoint

The September 11 dry-run inspection found 10 Senate notice PDFs and 17 House
notices in February 23–March 1. A Senate Appropriations Transportation/Tourism/
Economic Development “Not meeting” row had no stable notice ID, so it remains
an explicit qualification issue rather than an invented canceled event. The
Fiscal Policy notice 6880 contained 18 bills; the supplemental OpenStates event
contained only 12 and was not used as an authoritative complete agenda.

For September 11–25, the Senate directory yielded zero notices and the House
yielded one joint Legislative Budget Commission notice (15223), with no bill
agenda. These are source-window observations, not a guarantee that no later
notice will appear. Runtime reads must repeat this checkpoint from the deployed
image and record any source changes. Synthetic revision/cancellation fixtures
must not be presented as live qualification of a real revised/canceled notice.

The later daily-calendar qualification extends that initial directory-only
checkpoint. It discovers Special Order Calendar Group notices, Senate floor
days and the House's numbered regular-session floor sitting. Floor timing is
published evidence; ordered floor agendas remain explicitly unqualified.
Entries without stable publisher identities remain counted as unresolved.
Real cancellation notices 6892 (Senate, relative time) and 15187 (House) were
read. Senate Rules notice 6881 contains 45 agenda bills and an explicit noon
start. A real original/revised reschedule pair is still outstanding.

The September 11 joint sitting has two qualified notice identities: Senate
6914 and House 15223. An exact public crosswalk maps this pair once and exposes
both documents. The publishers disagree on regular-session classification
(Senate PDF 2027; House breadcrumb 2026), so the canonical session stays unknown
