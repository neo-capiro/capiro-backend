C.10 Audits and Information. Capiro will provide reasonable compliance information upon request, no more than once per year.

C.11 AI Training and Secondary Use. Capiro shall not use Client Content or Client Output to train AI Models shared with other clients without written consent.

EXHIBIT D: MINIMUM SECURITY MEASURES

- Access Controls: role-based access, least-privilege administration, prompt access removal
- Encryption: in transit and at rest for production data
- Tenant Isolation: logical segregation of Client data from other clients
- Logging and Monitoring: administrative access logging and monitoring for suspicious activity
- Personnel Controls: confidentiality obligations for personnel with data access
- Subprocessor Controls: written data protection agreements with material subprocessors
- Vulnerability Management: commercially reasonable identification and remediation of vulnerabilities
- Backups and Recovery: backup and resilience procedures
- Incident Response: procedures for triage, containment, and notification of Security Incidents
- AI Provider Configuration: controls, where available, to prevent third-party AI providers from training on Client Content
