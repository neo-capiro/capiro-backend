How it is built

Nothing on the screen claims more than it can show.

The same rules run through every module above. They are the reason a partner can put a Capiro screen in front of a client.

Provenance
Every figure names its source.

Dates carry the register they came from; filings carry their form and quarter; charts carry their datasets, row count and generated-at.

Derivation
Computed is never quoted.

A derived number is labelled as inference. If the server returns no reading, the strip is omitted rather than filled in.

Gaps
Missing is a state, not a zero.

Blank stays blank, unknowns are retained, unplaced funding is named, and “no material change” is reported as a finding.

Governance
The agent proposes; a person applies.

Meri drafts, reads back and proposes. Approval, export and send are separate actions, and every stage is gated and logged.

Under the hood

An enterprise platform purpose-built for the legislative environment.

Capiro runs its own data pipelines, agentic workflows and relationship graph — the parts general enterprise tools are not designed to support.

Intelligence engine
46 purpose-built AI agents.

One orchestrating agent managing 46 sub-agents, each with a defined scope, alongside 17 ML models running continuously — gradient-boosted classifiers, NLP attribution, time-series forecasting and graph centrality analysis.

Federal data

Congress, DoD, FEC, SAM.gov, USASpending, the Federal Register and LegiStorm, continuously ingested, with 1,536-dimension semantic embeddings so a program, precedent, staffer or submission is findable in seconds.
